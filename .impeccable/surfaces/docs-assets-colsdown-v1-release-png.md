## Direction contract

THESIS: The poster itself demonstrates a flexible column layout; copy belongs inside its geometry rather than under an unrelated illustration.

OWN-WORLD: Keep the cream, aubergine, purple, lavender, lime and orange identity and bold Geist lettering. Upright shapes, an active divider and nested geometry carry the mechanism.

STORY: Readers recognize Colsdown, understand it adds editable columns and stacks to Obsidian, and see four benefits: resizing, nesting, presets and direct editing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 136px title anchors the cream upper region; an orange 1.1.0 card on cream anchors the upper right, with no purple backdrop. The lavender rail has a draggable divider. The rounded central purple card contains two columns, with stacks nested inside one, and meets the lime preset rail without a vertical gutter. The lime preset column contains a plus and two miniature layouts. The lower editing region shows a caret beside rendered content. All four labels use 56px bold type, with 64px baseline spacing for multiline labels.

FORM: Modular broadside with native, editable SVG geometry and embedded fonts. Keep illustrations readable and labels inset within their regions. Use a shared soft shadow on the version, nesting parent/child cards, preset thumbnails and active editing card. Remove the former “Use it again” and arrow-key pitch; no bent navigation arrow. This is a static poster; web navigation, input states, motion and separate responsive layouts do not apply. Scale the full image proportionally at narrow widths.

FINISH: Verify full and narrow captures, confirm mechanism and type fit, obtain an independent finish review, and retain native-source provenance in the SVG, sidecar and exported PNG.
